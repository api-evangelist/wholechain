# Wholechain

> Wholechain is a supply chain traceability platform helping brands, retailers, and suppliers capture, share, and verify data across food, seafood, textile, and other supply chains — supporting compliance (e.g. FSMA 204), responsible sourcing, and consumer transparency.

## Product

- [Traceability](https://wholechain.com/traceability): Core platform for tracking products and materials through the supply chain.
- [Data Capture & Sharing](https://wholechain.com/data-capture-sharing): How data is collected from suppliers and shared across the chain.
- [Transparency](https://wholechain.com/transparency): Consumer- and partner-facing transparency tools.
- [Interoperability](https://wholechain.com/interoperability): Standards-based integration (GS1 and others) across systems.
- [Analytics](https://wholechain.com/analytics): Reporting and insights built on traceability data.
- [Integrations](https://wholechain.com/integrations): Third-party systems Wholechain connects with.
- [Compliance](https://wholechain.com/compliance): Regulatory compliance support, including FSMA 204.
- [Supplier Monitoring](https://wholechain.com/supplier-monitoring): Ongoing oversight of supplier practices and data.
- [Responsible Sourcing](https://wholechain.com/responsible-sourcing): Tools for sourcing verification and sustainability claims.
- [Food Traceability](https://wholechain.com/food-traceability): Traceability solutions specific to food supply chains.
